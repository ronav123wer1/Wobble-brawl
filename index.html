Controls

Tap a hand button to punch with that hand. Hold it to reach out and grab whatever you touch — players, props or ledges.
Grab with both hands, hold Raise Arms to lift them over your head, then let go to THROW.
Hanging off a ledge? Hold grab and press Jump to climb up. Being held? Mash Jump or punch to wriggle free.
Enough hits make you dizzy; too many and you're knocked out — easy to carry and throw! Out of the arena = eliminated.
